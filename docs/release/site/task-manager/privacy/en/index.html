Task Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Developer: Hoàng Dũng IT

This policy explains how the Task Manager app (Android, package io.github.operationd8979.taskmanager) handles data. In short: your tasks stay on your phone. The app has no account, sends your tasks nowhere, and uses no third-party analytics or crash reporting. The free Android version shows ads from Google AdMob; if you buy "Remove ads" through Google Play, the app makes no further ad requests.

1. Tasks and settings you enter

Task titles, notes, times, repeat rules, reminders and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and they are not backed up to the cloud. Uninstalling the app, or choosing Settings → Delete all data, deletes them permanently.

The app keeps a small error log on the device (error codes and times only, never task content) to diagnose problems. It never leaves the device.

2. Advertising (Android, until you buy "Remove ads")

Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google's disclosure, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's approximate location;
  • App interactions: app launches, taps on ads, video views;
  • Diagnostic information: performance of the app and the SDK, such as launch time.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Task titles and notes are never sent to the ad network. Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland. In these regions, Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Privacy options. Your choice is stored on the device in the IAB TCF format.

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). Buying "Remove ads" stops all ad requests.

3. Buying "Remove ads"

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; Google handles them under the Google Privacy Policy. To remember your purchase offline, the app stores on the device the purchase status and the transaction record Google Play returns (including the transaction token). This record is not sent to us and is not backed up to the cloud.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to remind you on time. Requested only when you turn reminders on.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to check purchases with Google Play.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android's Privacy Sandbox permissions): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in section 2.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Retention and deletion

We run no servers and keep none of your data. Data on the device is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Delete all data (your purchase is kept, because it belongs to your Google Play account). Data Google collects through ads or Google Play is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

7. Platforms

Task Manager is currently available only on Android, through Google Play. There is no iOS version. If one is released, this policy will be updated before it is.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
